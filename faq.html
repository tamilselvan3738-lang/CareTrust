Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our nurse vetting, insurance billing, scheduling flexibility, and clinical safety standards.

How quickly can CareTrust deploy a nurse or caregiver to our home?

In urgent post-discharge situations, our clinical coordination team can evaluate doctor orders and dispatch an RN or certified aide within 12 to 24 hours. For routine or scheduled care, we conduct an in-home intake within 48 hours.

What qualifications and background checks do your clinicians hold?

We accept only the top 4% of applicants. All nurses are licensed with their State Board of Nursing (RN/LPN), hold BLS/ACLS certifications, and undergo FBI fingerprinting, drug testing, and motor vehicle audits. Every aide is insured and bonded.

Does Medicare, Medicaid, or private insurance cover your services?

Skilled in-home clinical care (wound care, IV therapy, physical therapy) is frequently covered under Medicare Advantage, commercial health plans, and VA Aid & Attendance benefits when ordered by an attending physician. Long-Term Care Insurance (LTC) pays for companion and daily living assistance. Our billing specialists handle direct claims filing on your behalf.

What happens if our assigned caregiver is sick or unavailable?

We provide a 100% Guaranteed Backup Clinician protocol. Each patient has a primary and secondary paired clinician who is fully briefed on their medical plan, dietary preferences, and routines. You are never left without scheduled care.

How can family members monitor care updates?

Families receive secure credentials for our digital portal. After every shift, caregivers log vitals (blood pressure, glucose, oxygen), meals consumed, mobility exercises completed, and physician notes. You can check this from your phone anytime.

What is your minimum scheduling commitment?

We have a 4-hour minimum per visit to ensure clinicians can properly focus on clinical and personal needs. We do not lock families into long-term contracts; you can adjust or pause hours with 24 hours notice.

Have a question not listed here?

Our clinical coordinators are available 24 hours a day to assist your family.
